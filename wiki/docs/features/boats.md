A boat is not a five-plank craft you knock together in seconds. Like everything else here, it's a real
build — you make the oldest boat there is, a **dugout canoe**, by hollowing out a whole log. And once it's
on the water, it doesn't glide for free: **paddling is work**.

## Hollowing a dugout

There is no boat recipe. You work a real felled log:

1. **Set a log down**, then **sneak + right-click it with an axe** to *begin hollowing it* — the log becomes
   a **dugout-in-progress** in place. (Sneak is what tells this apart from ordinary axe-stripping; strip a
   log the usual way, un-sneaked.)
2. **Char the inside with a live flame** — right-click the dugout with a **lit torch** or a carried
   **ember**. The burn softens the wood.
3. **Scrape the burnt wood out with an axe** — right-click with any axe. It's **tiring work** (each scrape
   costs stamina), and the wood's too hard to cut until it's been charred, so you must char again between
   scrapes.
4. Repeat: **char, scrape, char, scrape** — **six cycles** hollow the log through, and it becomes a
   **canoe** (a boat). Set it on the water.

It's slow and laborious on purpose — a canoe is a serious undertaking, not a crafting-grid afterthought.
(The vanilla five-plank boat and bamboo raft **can't be crafted** — the dugout is the only way to a boat.
A chest boat you can still make the normal way, by adding a chest to a dugout you've already carved.)

## Paddling costs wind

A boat under paddle drains the crew's **stamina**. Keep moving and you keep tiring; let a spent paddler run
out and the boat **loses way and drifts to a stop** until they've caught their breath. So a long crossing is
something you pace, not a free ride.

**Two paddlers are the payoff.** With a second person aboard:

- The effort is **shared** — each paddler tires at *half* the rate, so a crewed canoe can cross far more
  water before anyone's spent (you can spell each other).
- Two put more into the stroke, so the boat cruises a little **faster** than one paddled solo (up to a
  steady cruising speed — you can't row it into a speedboat).
